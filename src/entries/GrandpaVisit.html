On Monday, Mom and Dad went to pick up Grandpa from the airport. I did my schoolwork while they were gone. After I finished my schoolwork, Amayi and I had a lunch of chicken. Once Mom, Dad, and Grandpa got back, I was super excited because I hadn't seen Grandpa in a LONG time. Once Grandpa arrived, we gave him a tour of the house before we went to pick up Chiboda so we could go to Game Haven. We got to Game Haven and everyone except Mom went on a game drive, because Mom had a meeting. We saw zebras-lots and lots of zebras! We saw kudu. We also saw wildebeest and impala. Then we ate dinner before heading back home and going to sleep.

On Tuesday, we woke up early so we could head to the lake. We had scones for breakfast and then went on a long drive. Mom did an amazing job. Once we got to Mphipe Lodge, we unpacked. Then we went to Sangalala for linner. I had my Double Trouble burger and the waiter said that that was my 'usual' and I ate it all. After we ate, we listened to a small band that had a guitar and two drums. I also figured out from that small band that for a guitar, you don't actually use your fingers. In your fingers, you have a plucker, and you pluck the strings with that, instead of using your fingers. On the way back to our lodge, we stopped so Grandpa could buy a few things as gifts. Once we got back to the lodge, Uncle Peter came and said hi before going out on his tour that he had.

On Wednesday, Mom, Dad, Chiboda, and Grandpa woke up SO EARLY so they could go fishing, because Uncle Peter had found a local fisherman that would take them to go find the best fishing spots. Amayi and I stayed to sleep. I was especially tired because I did not sleep very well. At 6:00, I woke up and I played Uno and another game for a while until Amayi was outside. When she was outside, Mom, Grandpa, Dad, and Chiboda were starting to come back. We could hear Chiboda yelling all the way from that boat, and we were on the beach. After eating breakfast, we got in the boat and went to see the fishing village, go see Mozambique, feed some fish eagles, and snorkel. The snorkeling might have been Grandpa's favorite part, because he didn't know that there were going to be so many fish, and he also didn't know that they were going to all be bright colors. After we had snorkeled for quite a while, we went to a beach on Thumbi island, a small island on Lake Malawi, and had a great lunch of chambo, rice, and ndiwo. Grandpa LOVED the chambo. After lunch, we went to feed some more fish eagles and snorkel some more. We fed one more fish eagle and we also threw some fish out for a hammercock. That was cool-it was their first ever trained hammercock. Then we headed back to the lodge for a dinner of chicken and chips.

On Thursday, we woke up, said goodbye to Uncle Peter, and then we went to Blantyre. We stopped at Chichiri mall so we could get KFC for lunch. Grandpa enjoyed it. After lunch, we headed back home and sat around all day long because we were pooped. Then, we had samosas for dinner and we went to bed. We were so tired.

On Friday, Dad and Grandpa went to go watch Chiboda's sports day event. He did really well, and won 4 events, came second in 2, and he broke 2 school records, which was really cool. Once they got back, we had zabwente for lunch--thick potato chips in batter. After lunch, Mom, Grandpa, and Amayi went shopping at the market. While they were gone, I talked to the electrician, told him what was wrong, and helped him a little bit. Once Mom, Amayi, and Grandpa got back, the power still wasn't working so the electrician came back one more time and replaced the main switch to get the power back on. That was great. For dinner, we had Mom and Amayi's specialty--drumsticks and chips.

On Saturday, I woke up to Mom making her specialty: bacon, ham, and gravy for breakfast. Dad and I spent some time together while Grandpa and Mom went to the museum of Malawi. Dad and I talked about science and then we got the terrible news that Mom's car had completely died while they were out. Mr. Godfrey brought them another car. Once they got back, Grandpa and I threw the baseball. We made and discussed the science about paper airplanes, and then flew them. Then we played paper football and threw the baseball again. We also played chess, which was lots of fun. Grandpa's very good. Once Amayi got back from church, Mom and Amayi cooked some phenominal malamba for dinner. It was delicious.

On Sunday, we went to church. After church, we talked to Chimwemwe about his grades and things, then we went back home. We picked up Amayi for the drive to Majete. We stopped to have lunch at Krazy Chicken before we officially began the drive. It was a spectacular drive. It was half as long as the minibus drive last time we came to Majete. We could actually see everything, which was awesome because we saw some great views. On part of the drive we were on a mountain, we could see everything below us. We could see the Shire and the twists and turns in it. You have to be high to see the twists and turns of a BIG river. Once we got there, we we unpacked our stuff, found our houses, and then we hung out until dinner time. For dinner, there was butternut soup, chicken kebabs, and an apple noodle. Then, we went to sleep.
