On Monday, I did my schoolwork and Mom worked on a few things and did her embroidery. Then, after finishing my schoolwork, I carted Banda, Fiona, and Mpimu around in the wheelbarrow. I took them up and down the driveway in the wheelbarrow. For dinner, Mom made dulac rice and Mr. David Elliot was coming. We talked with him for a while, and then Amayi and I made some bottles and put them in the freezer. We filled them with water and put them in the freezer so when they freeze up, we can sell them. Then, we went to bed.

On Tuesday, Mom and Amayi went shopping for a few things that they needed, and I stayed with Dad. I did my schoolwork and then for a while, I asked Dad how his game was going, so he told me to come and watch as his factories made more airplanes. I played and climbed trees all day. Then, when Mom and Amayi got back, we hung out.

On Wednesday, I did my schoolwork while Mom and Amayi did a bunch of crafts so Mom has examples to show the women in Kasungu how to make things, so they can get some income.

On Thursday, Mom and Amayi did even MORE crafts. After finishing my schoolwork early, I was inspired too. I took some old groundnut shells and taped them to my toes to make claws. I clicked all around the house. Mom thought they were so funny, and Dad heard me from a mile off.

On Friday, Mom and Dad went to print Dad's exams and then they took them to the people who will give them out to all the students. Once they headed back, I was done, done, DONE with my schoolwork. We decided to go to Kholo1 for lunch. As we were walking, there was a man who had a bucket of CHAMBO! Mom jumped on the chance of a new chambo man. We bought all of his chambo, so we can have it for dinner. Then, we walked to Kholo1 for lunch with quite high spirits. For dinner, we had a delicious meal of chambo, ndiwo, and rice. We were so so happy to have a new chambo man because we hadn't had chambo in over a month.

On Saturday, I slept in and read on my Kindle. Once I got up, Mom and Amayi said that they were about to leave on the biggest shopping trip of history. Mom's friends were about to come and we wouldn't be here for a week, so Amayi, Dad, and Chiboda needed to stock up on food because we would have the car. After they left, I carted the children in the wheelbarrow again, and I gave all the children sticks, to where they could chase me around the yard. That was lots of fun. When Mom and Amayi got back a few hours later, we ate a delicious lunch of grilled cheese sandwiches. One of Mom's sandwiches was all burned up, so Dad and I each gave Mom a half of a good one, so she wouldn't have to eat the burned sandwich. After eating lunch, Mom and Amayi went to Limbe to get even MORE food. I climbed trees and had fun before they got back. For dinner, we had chicken korma and luckily because the baseball game was early, so we got to listen to Texas A&M play against Mississippi State. We won, but barely--we won 7 to 6. Mom went to bed while Dad and I stayed up to listen to the game. When the game was over, I went to wake up Mom so I could tell her the score. Then, we went to bed.

On Sunday, we woke up and went to church. We had a great church service, and then we went to play. I saw all of the babies, including Star and Bright. Then I played with my other friends before we went home. Amayi had made lunch for us, even though the power was out. Eventually, Amayi's sister's son and his friend came to fix the sewing machine, and now it is up and running. Amayi is so excited.
