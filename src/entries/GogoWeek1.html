On Monday, Mom was waiting to go to the AIRPORT to get Gogo and Aunt Olivia! While Mom was at the airport, Dad and I threw the baseball some and we talked about science. When Mom got back, Gogo and Aunt Olivia were not with her! She had dropped them off at Game Haven, so she could come and get us, so we could all go to Game Haven for dinner. There were a bunch of wildebeest honking kind of like geese, but also kind of barking. It sounded like a goose coughing. The food was great! We went back home and all collapsed in bed. I get to sleep in a blow up bed in Mom and Dad’s room.

On Tuesday, we went shopping in the market and on the way, Gogo gave me a new book, so I was glued the whole way there. After getting some groceries, we met Mother Thandie at Restaurant Africana to eat and talk about some stuff. When we got back, for dinner, we had chambo. Sadly, the power went out. We told Gogo and Aunt Olivia that it would come back in time to do the dishes. We were wrong.

On Wednesday morning, Aunt Olivia, Chiboda, Dad, and I loaded up in the car to go to Mulanje Mountain. First we had to drop off Dad at MUST. As we were going, we listened to a book. When we got there, we waited a while, and then our guide came. We got our backpacks and started up the mountain. All of us were shocked when we got there before 1. Aunt Olivia said she didn’t care what time we got there, as long as we got there before dark. We got there about 1:00, so she was impressed. We were all super tired. It rained some, and Chiboda told us all a great story. Our guide told us some stories about the mountain.

Meanwhile, Gogo, Amayi, and Mom went shopping. First, they went to Bvumbwe market to get some produce. They got kholowa, bananas, and a few other things. Mom wore a new zitinje and carried the kholowa on her head through the market. People were impressed that the mzungu could do those things. They went back home and had zabwente for lunch, before heading in to Limbe. They ended up going ztinje shopping the entire rest of the afternoon, only leaving because it was time to get Dad from the bus. Gogo cooked chicken fried steak for dinner.

On Thursday, we started hiking down the mountain. The way to the waterfall was easier than the way up. It was more rocky and dry, which was easier than going through the bushes and things. The coolest thing was, most of the time, we were walking right through the clouds. Finally when we got back to the car, all of our legs felt like jello. It was as if we could fall down at any second. We loaded into the car and headed back.

Meanwhile, back in Blantyre, Amayi's sister, Aunt Mary, came to collect the zitinje and to measure Gogo for a dress and some skirts. After she left, they went to Kholo 1 (the chicken and chips restaurant) for lunch. After lunch, Amayi, Gogo, and Mom went to the National Museum. They got to learn about Malawian history and culture. They also picked up the special coffee that Aunt Olivia had wanted so badly. We were home when they got back. We had malamba and kholowa for dinner.

On Friday, Gogo, Amayi, and Mom went to the market to get things so we can give things out to a village in Kasungu next week. Aunt Olivia and I did stretches and drew pictures. After Mom, Amayi, and Gogo got back, we headed off for the lake. The drive was pretty good, but we didn’t make it far before we had to get diesel. That's better because there’s more diesel than petrol right now. After getting the gas, we hit the road. The closer we got, the worse the roads got. When we were really close, at one part, the car was tilted. It was a strong car, so it kept on going. We finally arrived and has a tasty dinner before going to bed.

On Saturday, we woke up and had breakfast and went on a boat ride. We had lots of fun snorkeling and feeding the fish eagles. Then we got back and had lunch at a restaurant called Sangalala. After we ate, we checked out some trinket shops and I got a super cool wooden fish eagle.

On Sunday, we headed back from the lake. We had a flat tire. While we were fixing it, the car fell on Chiboda. Luckily, he had no major injuries-just pain was all. Then we headed back home and had KFC dinner. We packed up the bags for Kasungu, and fell asleep fast.
