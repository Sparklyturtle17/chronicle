On Monday, we woke up at totally the wrong time. SO early. So we could go on a game drive. Once we got there, the man who drove us was the exact same person who was the driver last time. He was extremely excited to see me. He was ecstatic. We saw a bunch of types of antelope and a lioness, who did not even care about us. She just kept on walking. It was interesting to see that she limped a little bit. It was such an amazing game drive. Once we got back, we ate breakfast of fruit, and a full English breakfast. It was delectable. After breakfast, I swam and there was this lady who took swimming lessons, and she taught me some strokes. She taught me the butterfly, the sidestroke, and the crawl. It was so fun to do all of those. I learned how to swim much faster than just doing the dog paddle. For lunch we had fish and chips before going out on another game drive. We didn't go far-just to the watering hole, and we saw every kind of antelope that was at Majete. We saw kudu, eland, bushbuck, waterbuck, impala, and nyala. There were also baboons and warthogs. As we were leaving the watering hole, an elephant came. It was a big one! It had nice big tusks. We also saw several other elephants before heading back. For dinner, there was some kind of vegetable soup, Minnesota soup, and roasted chicken, with roasted potatoes and vegetables. We finished up with a cupcake.

**NOTE FROM MOM: It was minestrone soup.**

On Tuesday, we woke up, packed up, had breakfast, and got in the car. We got back to the house so everybody could change into nicer clothes to go to Chiboda's award ceremony. Chiboda got the Personal Academic Endeavor award, and the Victor Ludorum (sports) award. After we got home, Mom and Dad packed things to send back with Grandpa. Grandpa and I played paper football, and threw the baseball some. Mom and Dad also went to the shop to buy some delicious t-bone steaks that Grandpa cooked. They could have won at the Steak Country Fair. Then, we went to bed.

On Wednesday, Mom, Dad, and I said goodbye to Grandpa and Chiboda after we ate breakfast. Luckily they still had some time, so Grandpa and I threw the baseball and played one last round of paper football, and we played some little games. We didn't have enough room in the car, so I stayed with Amayi. Amayi and I worked and worked to clean out Chiboda's room. We found so much stuff, including grass. It was good to clean up Chiboda's room. Once Mom and Dad got back, we had bang-bang shrimp for lunch. We hung out in the afternoon, before we went to Elly B's to celebrate its reopening, and us only having 1 more month in Malawi. Elly B's has changed a lot. They put fake plants on top of the chairs, they have a different cover for their menu, and it feels like a completely different restaurant. Luckily, they still have the Angoni pizza, which is what Dad and I were looking for. We both ate our own pizzas. We went back home and went to bed.

On Thursday, I slept in and read my book. I did double math today in my schoolwork because I learned how to draw a blueprint to scale. I drew a reading room for a house. We hung out the whole rest of the day.

On Friday, Mom and Amayi went to Limbe to get supplies for Alinafe, after eating a breakfast of paneer and haloumi cheese. I like the haloumi better because it soaked up more of the sauce made, which was delicious, AND it was a little bit softer. The sauce was made out of honey that had been watered down, and a few seasonings. While Mom and Amayi were gone, I did my schoolwork. Then, I played with Fiona and Banda. I also read my book. For dinner, Mom made taco soup. It had lots of colors in it--red/brownish soup, the corn was yellow, and green peppers.

On Saturday, I slept in and layed in bed while reading my book. For breakfast, we had delicious scones. Mom and Amayi went out to the village and I stayed at home and had the genius idea to make a bow and arrow. I even found scattered sticks and broke them to make small arrows. My bow can shoot very far-maybe 10 feet. For lunch, Dad had chicken and broccoli, and I had taco soup. Then, I found things to do while Dad was working. I shot my bow and arrow, climbed trees, and read a little bit. It was a long, long, long day. For dinner, we had gravy potatoes which Mom and Amayi have really perfected. Then, we went to bed.

On Sunday, we went to church and Mom, Dad, and I had matching 4th of July outfits. We wore them today because Mom was gone yesterday. After church, we saw Bright and Star before heading out. When we got back, Amayi had made Pangolin Food, which Dad named. It was sausages and rice with carrots, bell peppers, and onions. Then we hung out and caught up on the blog.
