On Monday, Mom and Amayi were already awake and they were about to get ready to go on some errands. I stayed so then I could do my schoolwork. I did pretty well, and by the time Mom got back, I was almost done. Mom said that while they were shopping, they saw Ms. Justie. Mom thought that was exciting because they come to Malawi every month. We went to bed early because there was another baseball game in the morning!

On Tuesday, Dad and I woke up early to listen to the baseball game. Unfortunately, we lost though. After the baseball game, I did not go to bed. I went to my room and started reading. Then, sometime around 6, I got up, took a shower, and started up my schoolwork. Mom and Dad were still sleeping, so I went ahead on my schoolwork. After I was finished with math, Mom and Dad got up and headed out to MUST. They went to pick up Dad's final exams for his students. When they got back, I was allmost done with my schoolwork. After lunch, I finished my schoolwork and then we all hung out the rest of the day, while Mom and Amayi made notebooks. The notebooks are going to be brought to America and sold at craft fairs, at the Alinafe booth. They are going to have an Alinafe sticker on the back, so people can learn about Alinafe and raise money for Alinafe. For dinner, we had some delicious chambo, and then we went to bed.

On Wednesday, I woke up and started on my schoolwork. I finished super duper early and was super duper proud of myself. Amayi had gone out to her village, so she could see about purchasing a garden. Amayi was renting a garden on the very top of a mountain. It was such a long walk up the mountain, and in harvest season, the men would have to carry down big bags of maize. Nobody liked this garden because of the conditions. Amayi said she needed to buy a garden, so she went out to ask about a garden that was for sale. She thought it was great because it has a river running around the corner of it, and it was a really big one, so she could fit all of her crops. It is really close to her house, which is very good. For lunch, Mom made some delicious samosas. After lunch, I finished my schoolwork in a flash and so I got to play. I even beat my SpyFox game, and was jubilant. After playing my game, Dad and I also threw the baseball for a while. Then, we went back inside and I went back to my room and read for a while. THen Amayi came back and Mom made the most delicious chicken and paneer korma with green beans and carrots. After dinner, I got to talk to Gram and Grandpa for a while, and Chiboda even came. Then after talking to Gram and Grandpa, we went to bed.

On Thursday, I woke up and had some toast with Mom. Then Mom headed out to go to Good Samaritan, so she could go pick up Bright to go to the hospital. He had an appointment with the cardiologist. I finished my schoolwork quite early at 12:30. Then, Dad and I threw the baseball for a while.

On Friday, I woke up, had some cereal, and Mom and Amayi had leftover korma for breakfast. Then I started my schoolwork. For lunch, Mom made marvelous burritos. A while after lunch, Mom and Dad went to go to the bookstore so they could pick up some books for me. When they got back, I finished those books quite fast. I played the rest of the day. On Saturday, I woke up, took a shower, walked outside, and climbed the guava tree. Then I went inside to say good morning to Mom. We had toast for breakfast. Once Dad woke up, we went to ShopRite so Dad and I could get haircuts. While we were getting haircuts, Mom went shopping. I got a nice haircut and Dad got a really nice one. After getting haircuts, we went to Chicken Inn to get lunch. Mom got the Hawaiian burger and the rest of us gotr chicken and chips. Once we got home, we ate and then Dad adn I threw the baseball some. Then, when Amayi got back from church, she worked on her sewing project and a few notebooks. For dinner, we had orange chicken and I was so happy. Then, we went to bed.

On Sunday, I woke up but Mom and Dad were still asleep because they hadn't gotten good sleep. I read and played until they got up and we had breakfast. While I was playing outside, I wanted to climb a tree, but I saw this gigantic caterpillar walking down the branch. I sprinted back inside to go tell Mom, because she usually likes to see cool animals. We went to go see the caterpillar, but we had to wait a good 20 minutes poking around on the tree, because it was so well camouflaged-we could not see it at ALL. Eventually, Mom poked around on the tree with a stick, and finaly bumped into it, to where it started wriggling around. Then, she got it onto the stick, and made it fall down. Mom said it was STRONG because it was wrapping itself around the stick and fighting it. It was really cool. It was a cream color with some maroon bumps. I wanted to try to see how long it was compared to my index finger, but I didn't want to touch it because it has so many hairs on its body, and I thought those could carry poison. So I lined my finger up on the stick Mom was using, and at the very tip, made a mark with my pencil. Then I lined the stick up to the caterpillar and it was a tiny bit longer than my finger. It was huge with 16 legs. When we showed it to Amayi, to ask if she knew what it was, she said yep, people eat these. Mom looked mortified. She showed us a few pictures of people cooking them with ndiwo. She said these were a nice snack. Mom said NO. After playing with the caterpillar some, I went back inside, to try to copy a QR code in my math book. I am still trying to work on it. In the late afternoon, the water finally came back on, but it was a dirty, dirty brown. We let it run a minute to where it would come back crystal clear. The water was back, but the power went out, which was unfortunate because Mom and AMayi were in the middle of making gravy potatoes for dinner, which were delicious. After eating, we went to bed.
