On Monday, after we ate breakfast, Mom and Amayi went to the market to buy some stuff that we needed. When they got back, they said it was way cheaper than the shops, which was very good. After I finished up all of my schoolwork, Junior came. We played for a while and we sold a LOT of freezies today. I was really impressed with how well our business is going. Later, while Uncle Kenneth was cooking his nsima, I went and got a couple of guavas. I skewered them on a stick and put them on the fire for a little bit. The first one, I put in the fire for a long time, but it wasn’t directly in the fire-it was in the ashes. When I took it out of the fire, it was gooey and warm inside. The second one, I put directly in the fire, for a shorter amount of time, and the skin was black but it was a little crispier on the inside instead of warmer and gooey like a smoothie. The third one, I wrapped in leaves and put it in the fire. The leaves got burned up, but when I shook off the leaves, it was still blackened, but the inside was HOT. The leaves had burned up around it, and cooked it very nicely. The problem about this is, they are not very tasty.

Tuesday was Martyr’s Day. It honors the people who died standing up or what they believed in, during Malawi’s war for independence. For breakfast, Mom made delicious egg muffins. After that, I did my schoolwork. For lunch, Mom made the greatest lunch of popcorn and groundnuts. Amayi’s sister came, so she got to share the groundnuts and popcorn with us. After Amayi’s sister was done measuring me, she and Amayi went to the market. While they were gone, Uncle Kenneth and Uncle Peter were opening up one of the drains by the bathroom and inside we found one giant poop. I think it was mine because it had a lot of guava seeds in it. Mom was trying to make pizza crust, but she ran out of flour and couldn’t make it. So she transformed it into bread. Chiboda came home with a giant pinecone! He said he caught a girl from falling out of a tree at school, so she gave him the pinecone she was getting, as a thank you. For dinner, we had Dulac rice. I had eaten 3 bowls and was about to get up for another, but Mom stopped me before I could make it to the kitchen. That was kinda sad.

On Wednesday, Mom made scrambled eggs, and then we headed to Limbe searching for flour. We looked in every nook and cranny. Finally, there was a man who escorted us to a good flour shop, and it was so great-the price was much better than store prices, so we were all very happy. We also got popcorn and after popcorn, we went to the spice shop, which when you stand in the door and close your eyes and sniff, it smells so good because of all of the spices and herbs. The whole adventure is picking a packet up and sticking your nose into it and smelling it. After shopping, we wanted to go to lunch, because Mom had found a new restaurant which is by St. Andrews-a school that Chiboda plays rugby at. We took two searches, but we couldn’t find it, so we went to Chichiri Mall instead, and had Krazy Foods for lunch. After lunch, we went to ShopWise (ShopWrong), which is the buyer of ShopRite. They are open now! We got what we needed and when we went to check out, the line was super long. They kept having to type things into the computer because their system was new, and so we had to wait in line for 2 hours before we could even get to the cashier. We call them ShopWrong and they deserve the name because a)they didn’t change the name very much and b) their computer system was awful. When we went home, Amayi and I set out freezies to sell. We sold a few, and then Amayi walked over to a man selling clothes. There was this one blouse that she really liked, but she didn’t want to buy it. I ran inside and counted our freezie money. The shirt was 2,000MWK but we only had 1,700 kwatcha. At that point, Amayi was talking to her friend and not paying attention. I begged the man for a discount, and told him that the 1,700 was all I have had the whole time I have been here. I started to go away with a very sad and mopey face, and then he gave me the discount. Amayi was going inside, so I ran and then I gave her the shirt. She was so happy.

Thursday morning, while I was doing my schoolwork, Amayi told Mom that her aunt had passed away, and I was sad. After Amayi ate breakfast, she packed up her bag and left to go to the funeral. She will be gone for a few days. Part of my reading lesson was a hymn study. It was Abide by Me, one of my favorites. After I finished my schoolwork, Mom had gotten a cool new audiobook. It was called The Diary of Cleopatra VII, Princess of the Nile. It is great! People hoped to kill her father because he wasn’t being a very good king, so Cleopatra and her father went to Rome to get soldiers to fight if there was a revolt. It was very exciting and made you anticipate and worry about what would happen next. I also played a game of chess with Mom. She beat me, but I still had fun. While Mom was cooking, the power went out, which was bad. Even worse, when we were trying to do dishes, we realized the water was out, so that made it so much worse. So we went to bed without power or water.

On Friday, there was still no water, which reeked. I did my schoolwork and for lunch, we had chambo! Mom cooked it all by herself. It was delicious. I like the way Mom seasoned it. I spent the whole afternoon reading awesome new books that Mom had got on my kindle. They were great. I read Roller Girl, about a girl who played roller derby. It was a lot of fun reading the whole afternoon. Because we had no water or electricity again for dinner, Mom went on strike. We went to a new restaurant because EllyB’s was closed. It was sooo good. It was called Restaurant Africana. I got a burger which was delicious. Dad got a pizza and I tried a slice of it and it was SOOO good. Mom got nsima and chicken, and I tried a little bit of that and it was so so good! When we got home, we went straight to bed because ther was no power and our bellies were full, so we went to bed early. At 10:00, Chiboda woke us up to alert us that the water was back on, so we were so very happy. We were stoked.

Saturday morning, I woke up and pulled my loose tooth out. Now, half of my lost teeth have fallen out in Malawi. They were born in Malawi and wanted to die in Malawi. After that, Mom made some delicious french toast. Then Mom and I listened to our audio book and my zimbe wound finally healed, so I got to climb a tree again for the first time in a week. I was so excited about that. For lunch, we went to the mall and got KFC for lunch. That made us all feel better. What made us feel even BETTER, was Mom and I got ice cream, and Dad got a milkshake. I was ready to play and play and play once we got home. For dinner, we had chambo. This time, it was super good.

On Sunday, we woke up and went to church. I wore my special nkango shirt, which means lion in Chichewa. When we got home, Amayi was back! For lunch, we had some tasty ndiwo and rice. Then Mom and Amayi did their embroidery while I played with Fiona and Banda (Fiona’s friend). We had lots of fun. It was raining a lot. Then, Mom had to go out in the cold rain and move the car because I realized it was hailing. It was very small, but it was hail. While they were doing their embroidery, a sparrow flew into the house. We had to chase it around and close the doors, so we could shoo it to the door. While it was flying, it pooped on Dad’s hand. Mom made chicken and broccoli for dinner.
