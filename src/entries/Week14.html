On Monday, we had quite a lazy day. I did my schoolwork and the chambo man came! He brought a gigantic malamba that Amayi cooked up for lunch. We had zabwente for dinner.

On Tuesday, Dad went in to MUST while I did my schoolwork. For lunch, we had nsima and gumbo. It was delicious. For dinner, Mom made butter chicken!

On Wednesday, the chambo man came with a fish that really shouldn’t be on this Earth. It could be on another planet. I worked on my schoolwork while Mom did some things for Dad. After I finished my schoolwork, we walked to a new chicken and chips place. It was delicious. We all ordered chicken and chips. It was different from other places, because instead of being fried and breaded, it was cooked on the grill. We have a new favorite chicken place! While we were walking back, Mom and Amayi needed tomatoes for the super ugly fish. For dinner, we had the ugly fish. It was great, but mpasa is better.

On Thursday, we didn’t do much. Luckily the chambo man came, because he was going to go out to the village so he could harvest his maize. He knew we had visitors coming, so he dumped a whole bucket of fish into our sink for us, to last us. For dinner, we decided to go out to Restaurant Africana. After dinner, Dad and I got ice cream!

On Friday, we were waiting for the man to come to get us petrol. This petrol shortage really stinks. I did my schoolwork and finished early-that is always fun. I played and played and then when we sat down for lunch, the man came for the car. He went off and got us the petrol, and came back later in the evening. For dinner, we had the superb dinner of: fried chicken, fried telele, and fried potatoes.

On Saturday, I woke up coming into the kitchen with my nose smiling and being very happy, because Amayi was making mandazi for breakfast! Mom went on a great food hunt. While she was gone, Dad and I attempted to get the baseball game on his computer, which didn’t work. That was sad. Later, we went and threw the baseball and did some pitching practice. I also made a Chimanga Knight. I got some bamboo and made a sword, and I used maize leaves and cellotape. I love tape so much.

On Sunday, we ate some yogurt and granola before going to church. Today was apparently pastor appreciation day. All of us were confused because everyone kept going up and giving money and gifts. There was even one lady who took an nkhukhu (chicken) and gave it to one of the pastors. Then for dinner, Dad and I made jambalaya. For the first time cooking jambalaya in Malawi, the power did NOT go out, which was awesome!
