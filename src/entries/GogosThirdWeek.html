On Monday, Mom, Gogo, and Amayi went to the market so they could finish the list to go to Amayi's village. When they got back, we went to Amayi's village so we could pass out gifts and toys. All of the people were singing and dancing. It was a lot like the welcoming for Kasungu. While I was there, I taught some people how to do a handstand and taught people how to use the toys that they got. After a while, we went inside to eat a lunch of nsima, chicken, and beans. It was great. We hung out some more and then we headed home.

On Tuesday, it was Dad's birthday! We didn't really do much. We hung out and then we went out to MUST so Dad could give his lectures. After his lecture, we gave his students a big bucket of jambalaya. At first, they didn't have much, so we gave them bowls and once they tried it, we said there was enough for seconds and people started swarming. I think they loved it. For dinner, we had state fair subs and brownies for his birthday dinner. I made Dad a really good card.

On Wednesday, we went out to Limbe so we could give Good Samaritan a bunch of tangerines. We came back and had a lunch of chicken korma. Then we went out to Good Samaritan. We were super excited because we got to give Gogo a tour of the place where Chiboda and I came from. When we arrived, people were just coming from school, so it was pretty empty. While Aunt Olivia, and Mom were showing Gogo around, I had fun with other children throwing corn cobs at each other. There was a huge pile of them because it was harvest season and all of the people had picked all of the kerlens off of the cobs, so we threw them at each other. It was SO. MUCH. FUN. We had to hide behind our own barricades and constantly dodge corncobs while we were throwing at the same time. After the corncob war, we went to church. There was this one little baby who was poking and biting me, so I moved back like 6 rows and then he left me alone. Church was awesome. When we got back home, we had spaghetti and soya pieces for dinner.

On Thursday, it was my birthday! For my first present, I got a smartwatch that will track my steps and do other cool things. Right after I got it, Aunt Olivia and I went on a 2 mile run. I was super proud of myself for completing it. We hung out and I played with Dad and everybody then for lunch we had orange chicken. We hung out some more until for dinner we had sweetfire chicken, which was great. Then Mom and Gogo brought in a massive cake. It was so good. All cakes are. I'm 9 years old now!

On Friday, Aunt Olivia and I got up and went for a run. We came back to cake for breakfast and hung out until it was time for Gogo and Aunt Olivia to leave. That was sad. While Mom was gone, I hung out with Dad and then we took the rest of the day off.

On Saturday, it was a lazy day. We all needed it after the hustle and bustle of Gogo and Aunt Olivia being here. Amayi went to church. I completed my crystal frog that Gogo got me for my birthday. It was just a lazy day.

On Sunday, it was the same as yesterday. We didn't do much. We just hung around the house and I played with my friends.
