On Monday, we got to see Uncle! That was very exciting because I hadn’t seen him in a very long time. I asked him some questions about my mother and the Ngoni. I arm wrestled him–Uncle is VERY strong. After Chiboda got home from school, we went to Good Samaritan, which was fun. Mom got to see Bright and Star, which are two very little babies. I played with my friends.

On Tuesday, Mom and Dad went to meet Mother Thandie at the hospital, to meet with the Head Pediatric Cardiologist about Bright. While they were gone, I did my schoolwork and ate a mango. Mom and Dad got back after a little while, and she heated up leftovers for us for lunch. Then I went outside to play with Junior. We got to sell freezies to people who were walking. We got MWK1200 profit. Today was special because we have officially been in Malawi for one month!

On Wednesday, Mom had to wake up early for a meeting with Chiboda’s school, while I stayed home with Amayi, doing my schoolwork. In the afternoon, the chambo man came. Amayi had gone to the market, so Mom had to clean the fish. Every time she did a fish, she had to go out on the konde so she could breathe, because the fish guts were so gross. She even had scales in her hair. After lunch, Amayi and I made 107 freezies! We were all super excited about that. They were banana and mango flavored. It was so fun to make them. Junior came over and he was delighted to hear that there was 107! ESCOM was out so long today, and it only came back once for like 2 minutes and then was out again.

On Thursday, Mom and Amayi went out to go run some errands. I stayed here with Dad and did my schoolwork. Something very amazing happened. It RAINED! It hadn’t rained for almost a whole week! The maize had been drying up, so we were thankful. Uncle Kenneth was outside in the rain, and all he had was some cardboard, which would have melted in the rain. I ran a jumbo (plastic bag) out to him, because that does not melt in the rain.

On Friday, I did my schoolwork. For lunch, we had some amazing nsima and telele (okra) and it was magnificent! For dinner, we went out to EllyB’s, which was very tasty. Mom and Amayi got some chips and chicken with BBQ sauce, and Dad and I split an Ngoni pizza, while Chiboda had fish and chips.

Saturday morning, Amayi went to go see her parents for the weekend, while went to Thyolo. Thyolo is Chiboda’s village. The Good Samaritan car picked us up, and we got gifts for the village that the villagers appreciated. We got matches, cooking oil, salt, sugar, bathing soap, and laundry soap. Then, we all piled back in the car with Chiboda’s siblings and went to Thyolo! The drive was long and had a bunch of pot holes. We saw a lot of people and we drove past Dad’s university that he is going to work at and teach a class for. We also drove past Chiboda's old school. After turning down dirt roads that we weren’t sure if they were even for cars, or only people who were walking, we finally arrived. Mom and Dad had a meeting with the people there, and Chiboda went to go see his friends. I played with Innocent, one of Chiboda’s brothers. We tried to catch one of the little baby chicks, but they were too fast. Then we had a race. We took pictures of Chiboda’s village. We took pictures of the houses, the cornfields, and some of the animals. I also took pictures of the pumpkins that were growing. As we were getting ready to leave, the car broke down, so we had to fix it, which took a little while. By the time we got home, we were exhausted.

On Sunday, we all slept super late, and we didn’t do much all day because yesterday was very hard. For lunch, we went to KFC. We sat outside, and it started pouring. You might think we got wet, but the umbrella was good enough protection for us. When we got home, Junior came over! Mom and Dad put the laundry back out so it could dry. Unfortunately, about 10 minutes later, it started pouring again. Junior and I, with freezies hanging from our mouths, helped bring all the laundry into the living room to dry. Mom had put a blanket out on the konde, to dry. It was draped over some chairs Junior and I pretended that it was our house, and we crawled under it. We also played that the walk to school was 300 miles, and every time we were late, it doubled in length, so the first time we were late, it doubled to 600 miles, then 1200 miles, and so on. Then, I went to go get my swimsuits, so we could play in the rain. We made a slide out of the ramp, because it was super wet. We ran and slid on our bellies, and went super fast. Then I pretended that I was an old man, and we put my glasses right on the tip of my nose, and I put lotion in my hair to make it look white, and I had a mustache and beard made out of lotion. Next, Junior and I made our faces look like warriors with lotion war paint. We went outside after the rain, and saw a HUGE rainbow! Then we talked to the guards until Junior needed to go home, so we said goodbye. To dry our clothes, Dad made a sock tree.
