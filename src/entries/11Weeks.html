On Monday, we woke up, and Joy had died in the night. There was nothing in the fridge, so Amayi ran to the market. It rained all day long, which was kind of frustrating because we thought it was going to stop raining for a while. When Amayi got back, we had nsima and telele (okra) for lunch. It was delicious. After lunch, the chambo man came with catfish and campango, which is similar to catfish. He also brought some littler fish for Uncle Kenneth, because Uncle Kenneth does not care for malamba (catfish).

On Tuesday, Mom went to take Dad to the bus stop, and she didn’t come back because she had to go pick up Bright to go to the hospital. I did my schoolwork while Amayi cooked nsima. For lunch, we got to eat with Uncle Peter, and we had nsima, telele, and a boiled egg. It was great. After lunch, I finished up my schoolwork, but Mom still wasn’t back. Finally, after several hours, she came back when it was raining. In the evening, the Uncles came to thank us for the chambo that we gave them yesterday. They said it was a good dinner.

On Wednesday, I did my schoolwork while Mom worked on her embroidery, and Amayi had her hair extensions put in. She had them put into braids. The power went out, so we had samosas for lunch because they are easy to do on the gas stove. Amayi’s sister came to deliver some new clothes. After a while, she came back, because we needed more buttons. She was surprised that I knew how to do embroidery and how I sewed a button onto my shirt.

On Thursday, I did my schoolwork and I did a really good job and finished super early–11:50. For lunch, Mom made delicious pizzas! One of them had mushrooms and beef, and the other had only beef. After we ate lunch, Mom and Amayi ran to the store. I stayed here with Dad. For dinner, we had chambo. After we ate, we packed up for Lilongwe.

On Friday, we woke up in the morning and went on a six hour drive to Lilongwe! We went to Eden because that was a place that we knew pretty well, so we checked in and got house number 1! We liked it because last time we had been there for 3 months, that was the exact house we were in. After, we went to Chicken Inn for lunch, and then to Bishop Mackenzie International School for Chiboda’s basketball tournament. Chiboda played pretty well, and Victor came! Victor is Chiboda’s friend. When Chiboda’s tournament was over, we went with Victor to Casa Linda! Casa Linda is a delicious Indian food restaurant. It was still super tasty, just like we remembered. When we were done at Casa Linda, we drove Victor back home and found a shortcut to Eden.

On Saturday, we dropped Chiboda off so he could watch the girls’ rugby. Then we went on a great coffee hunt for Aunt Olivia’s most loved Mzuzu coffee. Sadly, we couldn’t find any. Then, we went back to pick Chiboda up, and we went back to Eden. When we got back to Eden, we went to the little restaurant. Dad got a pork chop, Mom and Chiboda got chicken and nsima, and I had a double burger that nobody thought that I was going to finish. I ate it ALL, and I only and 4 french fries left. It was pretty tasty. After we ate, we went to the pool. It was the pool that Mr. Poni had been building and building and building the last time we were here. I had scratched my name into it, but we could not find it. I went into the pool and it was shallow and took me a long time to find if there was a deeper end. The pool has a jungle gym in the pool, which is made up of slides and ladders. The best part was sliding down the tube slide on your belly and plunging into the water super fast. There was also a big inflatable slide. Once swimming was done, we went to EllyB’s for dinner.

On Sunday, Mom and I took a walk around Eden. When Amayi got back from her daughter’s house, we hit the road. It was super hot and Chiboda said my back was like an ocean because it was so sweaty. When we got home, we went to Krazy Chicken for lunch. I got chicken and chips, and so did everybody else except for Mom, who got a chicken sandwich. Once we got home, we were all super excited because we could take showers because we were all super sweaty. I took a shower and then Amayi and I tried selling freezies, and we got 200MWK. Then the power went out, so we went to bed early, with no dinner.
