On Monday, it was Chiboda's birthday. Chiboda was very excited. He went to school and had a school trip. They went to go visit the Yonse-Bo soda factory. I stayed here and did my schoolwork and Amayi bought a local chicken. I helped butcher it with Uncle Peter, and I took a few pictures. After lunch, we went to go pick Chiboda up from school because we were going to Game Haven for his birthday. We also took Chiboda's siblings. We swam. The water was freezing, so I didn't jump much. But I did do a few dives to see how long it took me to get to the very bottom of the deep part of the pool. After we got out, we were all cold, so Mom told us to run back and forth in the grass, and I sprinted like a cheetah. It helped so well, that after a few minutes, I was warm. Then we went inside and ordered our dinner. While we were waiting, Innocent, Vanessa, Caroline, and I played Uno. I had so much fun at Game Haven.

On Tuesday, I woke up and Mom and Amayi went to the market because they wanted more zitenje. I stayed and did my schoolwork and finished it so fast that by the time Mom got back, I was FINISHED! Mom had picked up Chicken Inn for lunch and it was appetizing. For dinner, we had the local chicken that we butchered yesterday and it was delectable.

On Wednesday, I slept in excessively late. When I finally got out of bed, I started on my schoolwork. Mom helped me a little bit and also helped Dad. For lunch, we went out to the magnificent Kholo and we got chicken and chips. Once we got back, Ib finished my schoolwork then I played the rest of the day. For dinner, we had malamba (catfish). We haven't had it in months! Dad was so ecstatic.

On Thursday, for breakfast, we had SCONES! We haven't had them in such a long time and I was gleeful about them. After eating breakfast, I smashed my schoolwork to bits. I played until it ws time for lunch. We had leftover chicken and dumplings. Mom's dumplings are exceptional. After lunch, Mom and Amayi went shopping and they got some books for me: a poetry book (Where the Sidewalk Ends), and the great illustrated classic The Prince and the Pauper. For dinner, we had potato soup. Dad and I didn't think it was going to be good, but it was something else!

On Friday, I woke up and Mom made ramen noodles for breakfast. Then I did my schoolwork and I knocked it out. In the afternoon, I played with Fiona, Banda, and Mpimu. Then, we waited for Ms. Janet to come. Once they got here, a very good surprise was there! Shammah came! I was super excited. For dinner we had dulac rice. Shammah and I played before going to bed.

On Saturday, we woke up early to go to Amayi's village. We had scones for breakfast. Once we got to Amayi's village, the adults had a big meeting for Alinafe. They are starting a new Alinafe team in Amayi's village. While they were talking, Shammah and I made hammers by pulling up chopped maize stalks out of the ground, and seeing if they had clumps of dirt on the end of them. Then we pretended to battle each other with our hammers. After the big meeting, we came back home and Mom made chicken ad broccoli for lunch while Shammah and I played. Amayi also cut up papaya and Shammah and I both gorged ourselves. It was Mom's birthday, which was exciting. After playing for a little while, I heard the news that we were going to Game Haven! We got ready and we went on a game drive and saw elands, zebra, wildebeest, impala, and a giraffe. And a waterbuck. We had dinner and I had a nice beef burger. While we were eating, Shammah didn't have anything to drink because he drank up all of his apple juice and there were no refills. I took his glass and poured the rest of my fanta into it, so he could have mine.

On Sunday, I woke up early so then I could play with Shammah before he left, because he was leaving soon. We had fun kicking the ball and then we had chigumu and tea for breakfast. After breakfast we played some more and said our goodbyes. For the rest of the day, I played. It was super cold so we stayed inside most of the day and I talked to Dad. Mom went to go meet a man from Good Samaritan and then got some groceries. Once Mom got back, we hung out, I read my book, and we had magnificent chambo for dinner. Then we went to bed under our warmest covers.
