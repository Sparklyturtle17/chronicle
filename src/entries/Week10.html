On Monday, I woke up and started my schoolwork. Mom said she was going to go with Amayi to the market, so they went to the market and got stuff we need. They got a lot of stuff because there might be a petrol shortage, so we have an extra supply.

On Tuesday, we went to Amayi's market--Bvumbwe market! On the way, she showed us her bank and the road where her house it. It was too muddy and rainy, so we didn't get to go see hoer house, which ws sad. Bvumbwe market was great. It was big, and it wasn't super busy. There wasn't anyone bothering Mom because she's a mzungu. While we were there, we met a few of Amayi's friends, and they talked to Mom in Chichewa. They were astounded when Mom replied in Chichewa. I think it's Mom's favorite market so far. After the market, we went to the tea plantation. It is huge. It was so beautiful, and it smelled great. When we got home, we had chambo for dinner. It was marvelous.

On Wednesday, I woke up early to do my schoolwork. I sat on the konde with Mom. After I was finished, Amayi's sister came with a baby bird! Serenity is it's name. It seems very weak. Amayi's sister also brought us new clothes. I like the work suit that she brought me, best of all. It is blue cloth, with reflective material on it. Almost like a firefighter's suit, just blue.

On Thursday, we woke up and found that Serenity had died in the night. I did my schoolwork SUPER FAST and finished at 10:23. While I was playing outside with Fiona and her friend Tandi, Mom called me inside because in 5 minutes, I was going to get a video call from Kenly. It was great. We talked for an hour! I was very happy because I hadn't seen her in a LOOONG time. I got to give her a tour of the house, and outside. Outside it was loud because Uncle Peter was chopping up the guava tree. He was chopping down a branch because it was touching the electric fence, and in the night it was buzzing. He chopped it down, and now there's a branch lying in the little river, but it's much easier to get guavas that were unaccessible. After I was done talking to Kenly, I brought the phone back to Mom with 4% battery, but the power had gone out, so Mom was disappointed and laughing at the same time. Mom and Amayi were making dinner in the dark. What they were making was, chicken tenders, fried okra, and Texas toast.

On Friday, I did my schoolwork while it was raining like yesterday, but the power was out, which was awful. We still had units, which is what we pay with, so we didn't know what was wrong. Eventually it came back, but we weren't very happy with ESCOM. For lunch, we had leftovers. After lunch, we went to the egg farm where Fiona's mother gets the eggs that she sells from. They are much cheaper than Sana or ShopWrong's eggs. While we were walking, I found a huge cocoon. I think it has already hatched because there was a big crack in it, and ants were crawling all inside it. When we got home, it started POURING. After it was done pouring, Mom went to go pick up Dad from the bus stop, and she got some potatoes that we had for dinner, as gravy potatoes. They were great.

On Saturday, the power was out again. The electrician came to fix it, and was successful! For breakfast, we had pineapple upsidedown muffins. Those. Were. DELICIOUS! They were delicious because of the pineapples! They would have been regular old muffins if they didn't have the pineapples on them. The pineapple was my favorite part. After breakfast, Mom, Amayi, and Chiboda went to the market, and they got what they needed. While they were gone, I read, and for the first time in a week, it was a sunny day!

On Sunday, we went to church and we saw Mother Thandie there, which was great. After church, we went to see the babies. Bright looks so much better. He is still small, but he is starting to gain some weight. When we got home, we had catfish for lunch! It was so good. Amayi had the speced just right, and it had a little kick in it. It tasted great. In the afternoon, I found a baby bird, and named it Joy. Dad said it was so sad, just seeing it hop around the driveway, trying to go somewhere. I love it so much.
