On Monday, it was the Malawian Independence Day! I got started on my schoolwork and Mom had a big grocery trip to go on. Dad decided that we should go with Mom. So we went with Mom to go shopping, and we got sooooo much food, because we had nothing. Amayi went to her village so then she could help with the annual cemetery clean up. When we got back, Mom made sausages and street corn for lunch. It was so good. Amayi's daughter and grandson came to stay with us for a few days. Ntambi is 2 years old, and loud, and a bit violent. Mom made gumbo for dinner. Ntambi said it was delicious (in Chichewa) at least 5 times.

On Tuesday, Mom worked on some things and I started on my schoolwork. After I finished my schoolwork, I played for hours, waiting for Ntambi to get back from seeing his mom's friend. Once they got back, I played with Ntambi the whole rest of the day. He is cute but naughty.

On Wednesday, I woke up and did my schoolwork. Amayi, her daughter, and Ntambi all went to the village today, to go for a visit. After I finished my schoolwork--very early--Mom, Dad, and I went to KFC for lunch. On the way back, we stopped at Savers Choice, so Mom could get pistolette buns for dinner. Later in the evening, when Ntambi and Amayi all got back, Ntambi and I played and had so much fun. For dinner, Mom made her sloppy joe pistolettes. They were delectable.

On Thursday, Amayi's daughter ran an errand and left Ntambi with us. I did my schoolwork, while Ntambi was constantly bugging me, and kind of being fun as a partner in my schoolwork. After I finished my schoolwork, Ntambi and I played until lunch. Mom and Amayi had made little potato fritters filled with cheese.

On Friday, it was a lazy day. We did not go anywhere. I did my schoolwork and played with Ntambi. For lunch, we had ndiwo and beef strips. Then we hung out the rest of the day.

On Saturday, we wanted to go to a little market, and Amayi's daughter wanted to run an errand, so we dropped her off and headed to Zomba. The place where the little craft market was, was right next to a lodge, so it was easy to find. It was small, but that makes it all the easier to sell the things. There was this lady who taught women farming and how to make biogas and homemade fertilizer. The market was a big success, because we were trying to find out if it would be a good choice to sell the things that the women in Alinafe are making. We ate lunch at the lodge before going back to Blantyre. I played with Ntambi and Mom made butter chicken for dinner. Mom had the Alinafe Board meeting, so she wasn't with us. After Mom's meeting, we prayed and I went to bed.

On Sunday, we went to church. The church service was long and loud. They were playing lots of music during a fundraiser for the choir to go to a conference in Ntcheu. The person talking about it yelled into the microphone, even though it was at full volume. After church, we went to see Star and Bright, and Chemwemwe even came. Bright was trying to smile. He can smile a tiny bit, but then it is just a tiny open mouth 'o'. When we got back, Amayi had made chicken and chips. Then I played outside and hung out the rest of the day.
